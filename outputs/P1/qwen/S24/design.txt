1) Domain Entities (Classes):
   - OlderPerson
   - MedicalCaregiver
   - Medication
   - EmergencyContact
   - HomeCareNurse
   - FallDetector

2) Single Responsibility of Each Class:
   - OlderPerson: Manages personal information, requests help, and tracks medication intake.
   - MedicalCaregiver: Manages caregiving tasks, receives alerts, and tracks medication intake.
   - Medication: Represents a medication with details like name, dosage, and intake status.
   - EmergencyContact: Stores emergency contact information.
   - HomeCareNurse: Manages home care nurse information and alerts.
   - FallDetector: Detects falls and sends emergency alerts.

3) Relationships:
   - OlderPerson has one EmergencyContact.
   - OlderPerson has one HomeCareNurse.
   - OlderPerson has many Medications.
   - MedicalCaregiver has one EmergencyContact.
   - MedicalCaregiver has many Medications.
   - FallDetector is associated with OlderPerson.

4) Public Methods:
   - OlderPerson: requestHelp(), takeMedication(), checkMedicationStatus()
   - MedicalCaregiver: receiveAlert(), trackMedication()
   - Medication: intake(), isTaken()
   - EmergencyContact: sendAlert()
   - HomeCareNurse: sendAlert()
   - FallDetector: detectFall()
