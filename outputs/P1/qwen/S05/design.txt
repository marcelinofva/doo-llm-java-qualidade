1) Domain Entities (Classes):
   - DataPackage
   - Tag
   - Registry
   - Publisher
   - Consumer

2) Single Responsibility:
   - DataPackage: Manages the data and its versions.
   - Tag: Represents a version tag for a DataPackage.
   - Registry: Stores and manages DataPackages and their tags.
   - Publisher: Publishes new versions of DataPackages.
   - Consumer: Views and downloads DataPackages at specific versions.

3) Relationships:
   - DataPackage has many Tags.
   - Registry has many DataPackages.
   - Publisher can create Tags for DataPackages.
   - Consumer can view and download DataPackages by their Tags.

4) Public Methods:
   - DataPackage: addTag(Tag), getTag(String), getAllTags()
   - Tag: getTagId(), getDataPackage()
   - Registry: addDataPackage(DataPackage), getDataPackage(String), getTag(String)
   - Publisher: publishDataPackage(DataPackage)
   - Consumer: viewDataPackage(String), downloadDataPackage(String)
