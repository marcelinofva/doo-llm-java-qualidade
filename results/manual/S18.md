## Histórias

- US1: As a Staff member, I want to Apply a Hold, so that I can prevent progression through the workflow or other actions in the system until the issue is resolved.
- US2: As a Staff member, I want to Remove a Hold, so that I can allow progression through the workflow or other actions in the system now that the issue has been resolved.
- US3: As a Staff member, I want to Schedule a Meeting, so that I can bring the required parties together at a specific date/time to address an issue.
- US4: As a staff member, I want to denote an application with a Final Action status. So that I can finish it.
- US5: As a Staff member, I want to Manage Hearing Calendars, so that I can track hearing-related activities and due dates.
- US6: As a Staff member, I want to Manage Affidavits, so that I can ensure compliance with the requirements prior to the hearing.
- US7: As a Staff member, I want to Send a Formal Response to Applicant, so that I can satisfy communication and records responsibilities.
- US8: As a Staff member, I want to Record an Internal Note, so that I can share information with other Staff collaborating on the record.
