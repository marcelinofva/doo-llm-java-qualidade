US1: As an OlderPerson, I want to receive a wake up call from ALFRED in the morning checking on me if I am ok.
US2: As an OlderPerson, I want to receive day and night rythm support with agenda reminders.
US3: As an OlderPerson, I want to use ALFRED to maintain my contact list in my phone.
US4: As an OlderPerson, I want to use ALFRED to manage my agenda.
US5: As an OlderPerson, I want to insert my meetings into ALFRED using speech.
US6: As an OlderPerson, I want to receive reminders on my appointments.
US7: As an OlderPerson, I want to receive a reminder from ALFRED on all the things I need to bring with me when I leave the house.
US8: As a SocialCaregiver, I want ALFRED to remind me of the things I need to bring to the person I care for.
