1. Domain entities:
    - Camper
    - Parent
    - Group
    - Activity
    - Attendance
    - Staff
    - CampAdministrator
    - CampCounselor

  2. Single responsibility of each class:
    - Camper: represents a camper with attributes and methods for enrollment and attendance
    - Parent: represents a parent with attributes and methods for enrolling children
    - Group: represents a group of campers with methods for adding campers and scheduling activities
    - Activity: represents a scheduled activity with attributes and methods for tracking progress
    - Attendance: represents attendance records with methods for logging and viewing attendance
    - Staff: represents staff members with attributes and methods for assigning positions
    - CampAdministrator: represents the camp administrator with methods for managing campers, groups, and staff
    - CampCounselor: represents a camp counselor with methods for taking attendance and tracking progress

  3. Relationships:
    - Camper belongs to one Group (composition)
    - Group contains multiple Campers (association)
    - Parent has multiple Children (association)
    - Staff has one Position (composition)
    - CampAdministrator manages multiple Groups (association)
    - CampCounselor is assigned to one Group (association)

  4. Public methods of each class:
    - Camper: enroll(), attend()
    - Parent: enrollChild()
    - Group: addCamper(), scheduleActivity()
    - Activity: trackProgress()
    - Attendance: logAttendance(), viewAttendance()
    - Staff: assignPosition()
    - CampAdministrator: manageGroup(), assignStaff()
    - CampCounselor: takeAttendance()
