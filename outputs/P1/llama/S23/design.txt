1. Domain entities (classes):
    * Person
    * Contact
    * Meeting
    * Reminder
    * Agenda
    * Alfred (assistant)

  2. Single responsibility of each class:
    * Person: represents an individual with attributes and methods for managing their contact list and agenda
    * Contact: represents a contact with attributes and methods for managing contact information
    * Meeting: represents a meeting with attributes and methods for managing meeting details
    * Reminder: represents a reminder with attributes and methods for managing reminder details
    * Agenda: represents a person's agenda with attributes and methods for managing appointments and meetings
    * Alfred: represents the assistant with attributes and methods for managing reminders, agenda, and contacts

  3. Relationships (association, composition, inheritance):
    * Person has-a Contact (composition)
    * Person has-a Agenda (composition)
    * Meeting belongs-to Person (association)
    * Reminder belongs-to Person (association)
    * Agenda has-many Meetings (composition)
    * Agenda has-many Reminders (composition)

  4. Public methods of each class:
    * Person: addContact(), removeContact(), addMeeting(), removeMeeting(), addReminder(), removeReminder()
    * Contact: getName(), getPhoneNumber(), getNotes()
    * Meeting: getStartTime(), getEndTime(), getNotes()
    * Reminder: getReminderTime(), getReminderNotes()
    * Agenda: addMeeting(), removeMeeting(), addReminder(), removeReminder()
    * Alfred: remindPerson(), manageAgenda(), manageContacts()
