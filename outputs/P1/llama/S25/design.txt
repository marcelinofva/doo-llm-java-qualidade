1. Domain entities (classes):
    * Person
    * Door
    * Phone
    * Light
    * Heating
    * TV
    * Radio
    * MoviePlayer
    * NewsReader
    * GroceryShopper
    * Alfred (assistant)

  2. Single responsibility of each class:
    * Person: represents an older person with attributes and methods for interacting with ALFRED
    * Door: represents a door with attributes and methods for opening and closing
    * Phone: represents a phone with attributes and methods for making and receiving calls
    * Light: represents a light with attributes and methods for turning on and off
    * Heating: represents a heating system with attributes and methods for controlling temperature
    * TV: represents a TV with attributes and methods for turning on and off
    * Radio: represents a radio with attributes and methods for turning on and off
    * MoviePlayer: represents a movie player with attributes and methods for playing movies
    * NewsReader: represents a news reader with attributes and methods for reading news
    * GroceryShopper: represents a grocery shopper with attributes and methods for shopping
    * Alfred: represents the ALFRED assistant with attributes and methods for interacting with the older person

  3. Relationships (association, composition, inheritance):
    * Person has a relationship with Door (association)
    * Person has a relationship with Phone (association)
    * Person has a relationship with Light (association)
    * Person has a relationship with Heating (association)
    * Person has a relationship with TV (association)
    * Person has a relationship with Radio (association)
    * Person has a relationship with MoviePlayer (association)
    * Person has a relationship with NewsReader (association)
    * Person has a relationship with GroceryShopper (association)
    * Alfred has a relationship with Person (association)

  4. Public methods of each class:
    * Person: openDoor(), pickUpPhone(), turnOnLight(), turnOffLight(), controlHeating(), controlTV(), controlRadio(), playMovie(), readNews(), shopGroceries()
    * Door: open(), close()
    * Phone: makeCall(), receiveCall()
    * Light: turnOn(), turnOff()
    * Heating: turnOn(), turnOff()
    * TV: turnOn(), turnOff()
    * Radio: turnOn(), turnOff()
    * MoviePlayer: playMovie()
    * NewsReader: readNews()
    * GroceryShopper: shopGroceries()
    * Alfred: interactWithPerson()
