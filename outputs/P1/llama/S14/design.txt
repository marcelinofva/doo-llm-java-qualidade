1. Domain entities (classes):
    * NewsItem
    * NewsFeed
    * SiteMember
    * SiteEditor
    * NewsSite

  2. Single responsibility of each class:
    * NewsItem: represents a single news item with its attributes and behavior
    * NewsFeed: manages the RSS feed of news items
    * SiteMember: represents a site member with their attributes and behavior
    * SiteEditor: represents a site editor with their attributes and behavior
    * NewsSite: represents the news site with its attributes and behavior

  3. Relationships (association, composition, inheritance):
    * NewsItem has a one-to-many relationship with NewsFeed (a news item can be part of multiple feeds)
    * SiteMember has a one-to-one relationship with SiteEditor (a member can be an editor)
    * NewsSite has a one-to-many relationship with NewsItem (a site can have multiple news items)
    * NewsItem has a composition relationship with NewsFeed (a news item is composed of a feed)

  4. Public methods of each class:
    * NewsItem: getNewsItem(), setNewsItem(), getStartDate(), setStartDate(), getEndDate(), setEndDate()
    * NewsFeed: getNewsFeed(), setNewsFeed(), addNewsItem(), removeNewsItem()
    * SiteMember: getSiteMember(), setSiteMember(), getNewsFeed(), setNewsFeed()
    * SiteEditor: getSiteEditor(), setSiteEditor(), getNewsFeed(), setNewsFeed()
    * NewsSite: getNewsSite(), setNewsSite(), addNewsItem(), removeNewsItem()
