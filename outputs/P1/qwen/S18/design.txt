1) Domain Entities (Classes):
   - Hold
   - Meeting
   - Hearing
   - Affidavit
   - Response
   - Note

2) Single Responsibility of Each Class:
   - Hold: Manages the hold status of an application.
   - Meeting: Manages scheduling and tracking of meetings.
   - Hearing: Manages hearing-related activities and due dates.
   - Affidavit: Manages affidavits for compliance.
   - Response: Manages formal responses to applicants.
   - Note: Manages internal notes for collaboration.

3) Relationships:
   - Hold is associated with an application.
   - Meeting is associated with an application.
   - Hearing is associated with an application.
   - Affidavit is associated with an application.
   - Response is associated with an application.
   - Note is associated with an application.

4) Public Methods:
   - Hold: applyHold(), removeHold()
   - Meeting: scheduleMeeting(), cancelMeeting()
   - Hearing: scheduleHearing(), cancelHearing()
   - Affidavit: submitAffidavit(), validateAffidavit()
   - Response: sendResponse()
   - Note: recordNote()
