1) Domain Entities (Classes):
   - OlderPerson
   - SocialCaregiver
   - Alfred
   - Agenda
   - ContactList
   - Reminder
   - Meeting

2) Single Responsibility of Each Class:
   - OlderPerson: Manages personal information and preferences.
   - SocialCaregiver: Manages care for an older person.
   - Alfred: Provides assistance, reminders, and manages the agenda.
   - Agenda: Stores and manages appointments and meetings.
   - ContactList: Manages the contact list.
   - Reminder: Manages reminders for appointments and tasks.
   - Meeting: Represents a meeting with details.

3) Relationships:
   - OlderPerson has an Agenda and a ContactList.
   - SocialCaregiver has an OlderPerson and an Agenda.
   - Alfred interacts with OlderPerson, SocialCaregiver, Agenda, ContactList, and Reminder.
   - Agenda has multiple Meetings.
   - Reminder is associated with an Agenda or Meeting.

4) Public Methods:
   - OlderPerson: addContact, getAgenda, receiveReminder
   - SocialCaregiver: addCaregiver, getAgenda, receiveReminder
   - Alfred: wakeUpCall, manageAgenda, insertMeeting, receiveReminder
   - Agenda: addMeeting, removeMeeting, getUpcomingMeetings
   - ContactList: addContact, removeContact, getContacts
   - Reminder: setReminder, removeReminder, getReminders
   - Meeting: setDetails, getDetails
