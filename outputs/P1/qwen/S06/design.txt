1) Domain Entities (Classes):
   - Publisher
   - User
   - Role (enum)
   - Invitation

2) Single Responsibility:
   - Publisher: Manages members and roles.
   - User: Represents a user with an email and role.
   - Role: Enumerates different roles (OWNER, MEMBER).
   - Invitation: Represents an invitation to join a publisher.

3) Relationships:
   - Publisher has a list of Users.
   - Publisher has a list of Invitations.
   - User has a Role.

4) Public Methods:
   - Publisher: editProfile, inviteUser, inviteByEmail, removeMember, viewMembers, makeOwner, removeOwner
   - User: (no public methods)
   - Role: (no public methods)
   - Invitation: (no public methods)
