<design>
1) Domain Entities (Classes):
   - Customer
   - Applicant
   - Application
   - Document
   - Fee
   - Transaction
   - Staff
   - Review

2) Single Responsibility of Each Class:
   - Customer: Manages customer information and portal access.
   - Applicant: Manages application details and transactions.
   - Application: Represents a service request and its status.
   - Document: Represents supporting documents for an application.
   - Fee: Manages fee details and payment.
   - Transaction: Tracks the status of a transaction.
   - Staff: Manages staff actions and reviews.
   - Review: Handles completeness review and detailed review.

3) Relationships:
   - Customer has one or many Applications.
   - Applicant has one or many Applications.
   - Application has one or many Documents.
   - Application has one Fee.
   - Application has one Transaction.
   - Staff performs Completeness Review and Detailed Review on Applications.

4) Public Methods:
   - Customer: createAccount(), login(), logout().
   - Applicant: submitApplication(), submitDocument(), payFee(), checkStatus(), modifyApplication().
   - Application: getStatus(), isComplete(), assignForReview().
   - Document: uploadDocument().
   - Fee: getAmount(), pay().
   - Transaction: getStatus().
   - Staff: performCompletenessReview(), assignForDetailedReview().
</design>
