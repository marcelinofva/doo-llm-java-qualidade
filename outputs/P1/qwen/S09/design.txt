1) Domain Entities (Classes):
   - Collection
   - Item
   - Component
   - RemoteReplicationPolicy
   - RepositoryManager
   - CollectionCurator
   - Dashboard
   - EncryptionService

2) Single Responsibility:
   - Collection: Manages collection metadata and items/components.
   - Item: Represents an item within a collection.
   - Component: Represents a component of an item.
   - RemoteReplicationPolicy: Defines replication policies for collections.
   - RepositoryManager: Manages replication policies and encryption.
   - CollectionCurator: Manages collection replication and encryption.
   - Dashboard: Provides summary and statistics for collections.
   - EncryptionService: Handles encryption and checksum generation.

3) Relationships:
   - Collection has many Items and Components.
   - RepositoryManager has many RemoteReplicationPolicies.
   - CollectionCurator has many Collections.
   - Dashboard has many Collections.
   - EncryptionService is used by RepositoryManager and CollectionCurator.

4) Public Methods:
   - Collection: addItem, addComponent, getReplicationStatus
   - Item: getName, getComponents
   - Component: getName, getData
   - RemoteReplicationPolicy: getPolicy, setPolicy
   - RepositoryManager: setReplicationPolicy, encryptFile
   - CollectionCurator: queueForReplication, getReplicationSummary
   - Dashboard: getCollectionSummary, getDepositActivity
   - EncryptionService: encrypt, generateChecksum
